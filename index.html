How it works

The game stores restricted words as hashes. We compare names and their fragments with that list.

What gets checked

We check your whole entry, then its fragments, across all language lists. Fragments can cross spaces; first and last names aren’t treated separately.

Limits and privacy

A match may not apply to every language or naming context. Built for Steam build 26175237; updated lists may differ and non-ASCII results are approximate. Definition requests send flagged text to Dictionary API. English meanings do not explain why the game restricts a word.
